I — VI · The Programmes

Six programmes. One standard.

From a single fixture to a whole-home rebuild, every Crown visit follows the same discipline — a senior tradesman on site, master-grade materials, a written record of the work.

§ I. — The Inspection

The Diagnosticvisit & report

Ninety minutes on site. A senior plumber walks every fixture, scopes the main drain with camera, takes pressure readings at the meter and at the furthest run, and leaves a written report in your inbox by end of day. The fee is fixed before we arrive — and credited toward any subsequent programme should you proceed.

  • Whole-home fixture walkthrough
  • Camera scope of the main drain to the connection
  • Static and dynamic pressure readings
  • Hot water tank / tankless assessment
  • Written report with photos and prioritised findings
  • Fee credited toward any subsequent programme
§ II. — The Repair

The Single-Fixtureremedy

The drip you've been listening to. The toilet that won't quite stop. The shutoff that seized last winter. We diagnose the root cause — never just the symptom — and pressure-test the repair before we sign off.

  • Faucet & valve cartridge rebuilds or replacement
  • Toilet diagnostics, flapper, fill valve, full reseat
  • Shutoff valve replacement, supply line refresh
  • Garbage disposal swap or removal
  • Drain unclogs — kitchen, bath, laundry
  • Leak isolation behind wall or below floor
§ III. — The Refresh

The Fixtureinstall

You've chosen the hardware. We make it sit correctly. Faucets aligned to the millimetre, drains levelled, escutcheons true, silicones laid in a single pass. The room comes back commissioned — pressure-tested and ready to use.

  • Full bathroom hardware swap — faucet, toilet, shower trim
  • Vanity install with new shutoffs and supply
  • Kitchen faucet & pull-down sprayer install
  • Filtered water dispenser plumbing
  • Tile-side trim install on existing rough-in
  • Old fixtures hauled away
§ IV. — The Restoration

Repipes &drain replacement

For homes where the bones are tired. Galvanised, polybutylene, cast-iron drain stacks — we draw the system back to copper or PEX on the supply side, ABS on the drain side. Walls opened cleanly, lines pressure-tested at 1.5× operating, walls closed by our finishing crew.

  • Whole-home copper or PEX repipe
  • ABS drain & vent stack replacement
  • Sewer line replacement — open trench or trenchless
  • Main shut-off upgrade & pressure-reducing valve
  • Hose bib & exterior line replacement
  • Drywall opened, capped and ready for finisher
§ V. — The Renovation

Rough-in tofinish

A full bathroom or kitchen plumbing programme, coordinated with your designer, GC and tile-setter. We arrive twice — once for the rough-in (inspected and passed before the walls close), once for the finish. The schedule we give you is the schedule we keep.

  • Bathroom rough-in: supply, drain, vent, tub or shower drain
  • Kitchen rough-in: island, dishwasher, fridge line, disposal
  • Permit drawings & municipal coordination
  • Inspection-pass guarantee on rough-in
  • Steam shower & body-jet plumbing
  • Heated floor manifold (with HVAC sub-trade)
§ VI. — The Estate

The whole-homeprogramme

Custom builds and estate renovations on the scale that needs a master to live on site. A senior crew is assigned and stays assigned. One project lead. One quoted price. One signed handover at commissioning — with the manifold, the riser diagram and the maintenance schedule bound and delivered.

  • Multi-bathroom & multi-storey plumbing design
  • Manifold systems with zoned shut-offs
  • Recirculating hot water & tankless cascades
  • Water treatment, softener & RO integration
  • Pool, spa & outdoor kitchen plumbing
  • Documented commissioning binder on handover
