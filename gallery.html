§ Selected Work · I — VI

Six moments. One standard.

Every Crown engagement is documented — pressure readings, photographs, written report. What follows is the shape of the work itself, programme by programme.

◆
§ I. — The Diagnostic

A Gauge /at the manifold.

Ninety minutes on site, every fixture walked, the main drain scoped from the access stack to the city connection. Static and dynamic pressure read at the meter, at the manifold, and at the furthest run. The report — photographs, readings, prioritised findings — leaves with the homeowner the same day.

Duration · 90 min Tools · HD Scope, Manometer Output · Bound Written Report
§ II. — The Repair

Hands Under /the sink.

Single-fixture failures, hidden leaks, the diagnostic that turns into a repair before the visit ends. Quoted on site, fixed on site, pressure-tested at 1.5× operating before the kit goes back in the van.

Duration · 2–3 hrs Test · 1.5× Operating Warranty · Lifetime Workmanship
§ III. — The Refresh

Brass, Set /true.

Faucets, fixtures, vanity trim — supplied through us or by the homeowner. The room is depressurised, re-installed, re-commissioned, and tested with the homeowner present. Half-day turnaround for most fixture swaps.

Duration · Half-day Suppliers · Brizo, Kohler, Waterworks Sign-off · On-site Demo
§ IV. — The Restoration

Solder, Drawn /at the joint.

When aged supply lines need full replacement — copper drawn through the wall cavities, every joint torched and pressure-tested at one and a half times working pressure before the drywall goes back. Documented to the millimetre.

Materials · Type-L Copper, ABS Test · 1.5× Operating, 24 hr Hold Photo log · Every Joint
§ V. — The Renovation

A Bathroom, /commissioned.

Full rough-in and finish plumbing coordinated with the general contractor — schematics drafted for permit, inspections managed, fixtures landed and re-commissioned. The room is signed off by the master on the job, not a sub.

Duration · 3–5 days Permits · Managed Hand-off · Master Sign-off
§ VI. — The Estate

The Whole /house, manifolded.

Custom builds and estate renovations — multi-bathroom, multi-storey, with manifold systems, zoned shut-offs, and recirculating hot water designed before the slab is poured. A senior crew is assigned and stays assigned. One signed handover.

Scope · Whole-home Design Crew · Senior, Dedicated Hand-off · Commissioning Binder
