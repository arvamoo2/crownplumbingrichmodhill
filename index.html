01 / 06 · Discipline

Not a plumber. A craftsman's standard.

Our Approach

Every line we run, every fitting we land, every solder we draw — it's done as if our own name were stamped on the wall behind it. Because, quietly, it is.

We don't run vans. We run programmes. A senior tradesman owns the visit from quote to commissioning, and the home gets a written record of what was done, what we found, and what to watch for.

It's a slower way to plumb. It's the only one we know.
